# Sample Seed OOKRST Tree — ShiftLoop

> A **deliberately flawed** OOKRST tree (Opportunity → Outcome → Key Result → Solution → Test) for training. It is *not* a model answer — it has planted structural mistakes for Module 2's health-check exercise to catch (and for Module 3 to clean up when mapping in real signals). Do not copy this as a template. The facilitator key lists the planted flaws; don't read it until after you've done the health check yourself. Structure rules: [OOKRST Structure guide](../../guides/ookrst-structure.md).

## The tree

**OPP-1 — "Managers struggle to get their existing team and staff availability into ShiftLoop, because both live in spreadsheets and text messages they re-enter by hand."**

- **OUT-1 — Trial managers publish a complete first weekly schedule within their first week.**
  - **OUT-1-KR-1:** Raise 7-day trial activation (first complete weekly schedule published) from **38% → 60%** by end of quarter.
    - SOL-1: Spreadsheet import with automatic column mapping
      - TST-1: Concierge — manually import the staff list for 5 trial accounts and watch whether they publish (success ≥3 of 5 publish in 7 days; kill ≤1 of 5)
    - SOL-2: Bulk add / paste-a-list entry
    - SOL-3: Org-level roster push (owner loads all locations' staff at once)
    - SOL-4: Availability reminder nudges to staff
    - SOL-5: One-tap availability quick-entry
    - SOL-9: Tips & earnings dashboard in the staff app *(addresses OPP-4)*
  - **OUT-1-KR-2:** Ship the new import wizard by end of quarter.
  - SOL-6: Redesign the onboarding wizard *(no parent KR)*

- **OUT-2 — Reduce monthly churn of paying accounts.**
  - **OUT-2-KR-1:** Cut monthly paid-account churn from **4.1% → 3.0%**.
    - SOL-7: Loyalty discount after 6 months
    - SOL-8: Win-back email sequence for cancelled accounts

**OPP-2 — "I don't trust the auto-scheduler enough to publish what it makes."** *(no Outcome yet)*
- Ideas: show the reasoning behind each shift placement; guided "review & publish your first schedule" flow

**OPP-3 — Build a mobile app for staff**
- Ideas: push notifications for new shifts; shift-swap between staff

**OPP-4 — "My staff keep asking to see their tips and pay in the app."** *(no Outcome yet)*
