# PM Signal Synthesis

Use the signals supplied in conversation and relevant records from the host-injected active workspace. Cluster by underlying customer need rather than surface topic.

For every cluster, provide a customer-voice Opportunity statement, supporting evidence IDs, contradictions, missing evidence, and confidence: Strong for at least three independent consistent sources, Medium for two sources or one corroborated by data, and Weak for one source. Signals attach to Opportunities (and to the Solution or Test they bear on), never to key results.

Search existing Compass Opportunities before proposing a new one. Recommend linking to or enriching an existing Opportunity when meaningfully equivalent, and note which Outcome sits above it (or that none does yet). Creating a new Opportunity branch or changing focus requires the user's product judgment. When authorized, use host-provided Compass MCP tools and preserve source attribution.
