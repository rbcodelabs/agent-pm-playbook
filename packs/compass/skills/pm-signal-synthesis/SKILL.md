# PM Signal Synthesis

Use the signals supplied in conversation and relevant records from the host-injected active workspace. Cluster by underlying customer need rather than surface topic.

For every cluster, provide a customer-voice opportunity statement, supporting evidence IDs, contradictions, missing evidence, and confidence: Strong for at least three independent consistent sources, Medium for two sources or one corroborated by data, and Weak for one source.

Search existing Compass opportunities before proposing a new one. Recommend linking to or enriching an existing opportunity when meaningfully equivalent. Creating a new opportunity branch or changing focus requires the user's product judgment. When authorized, use host-provided Compass MCP tools and preserve source attribution.
